## Objectives

- Present the AutoClean AI concept through a concise and structured elevator pitch.
- Communicate the problem statement, intended impact, and practical value of the proposed system.
- Present the project's advantages, disadvantages, proposed technology stack, future scope, and anticipated technical challenges.
- Establish a clear justification for developing AutoClean AI within the course timeline.

## Work Done

- Prepared and delivered an elevator pitch for **AutoClean AI**, an automated data preprocessing system for machine learning datasets.
- Coordinated the presentation so that each team member presented a part of the project pitch.
- Structured the pitch around the following components:
  - **Problem Statement:** Challenges associated with manual, repetitive, and potentially error-prone data preprocessing.
  - **Expected Impact:** Reduction in preprocessing effort and improved transparency in data preparation.
  - **Advantages:** Automation of common preprocessing tasks, reduced manual intervention, and greater consistency.
  - **Disadvantages:** Risk of inappropriate transformations, loss of useful information, and limitations in handling dataset-specific requirements.
  - **Technology Stack:** Presented the proposed technologies and tools for implementation.
  - **Future Scope:** Potential extensions to support additional preprocessing operations and more diverse datasets.
  - **Anticipated Difficulties:** Handling outliers, selecting appropriate transformations, preserving data quality, and validating preprocessing results.
- Personally presented the problem statement and the expected impact of AutoClean AI.

## Decisions and Rationale

- Retained the technology stack presented during the elevator pitch, as no changes were identified as necessary following the presentation.
- Continued with AutoClean AI as the proposed project.
- Emphasized the need to communicate concrete user problems and measurable benefits rather than relying on broad descriptions of data-quality issues.

## Issues and Blockers

- No feedback was received on the elevator pitch.
- No questions or concerns were raised during the presentation.
- No immediate blockers were identified at this stage.

## Learnings

- An effective elevator pitch should communicate the problem, proposed solution, and expected impact clearly within a limited time.
- The problem statement and expected impact provide the primary justification for the project and should be specific and relevant to the target users.
- For AutoClean AI, the problem should be framed around concrete challenges such as repetitive preprocessing, limited transparency into applied transformations, and the risk of accidental information loss.
- Automation alone does not guarantee better data quality; preprocessing decisions must be appropriate for the dataset and should be validated.
- Presenting disadvantages and anticipated difficulties alongside the advantages provides a more balanced assessment of the project's feasibility.
- A clear explanation of technical challenges helps establish realistic expectations for the initial project scope.
